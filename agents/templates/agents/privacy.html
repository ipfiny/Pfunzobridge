Learner Privacy & Data Protection

PfunzoBridge is built for South African high school learners. We believe your educational data belongs to you. Here is our plain-English policy on how we respect and protect your personal information.

POPIA Compliance (Made Simple)

1

Ask for Permission First (Lawful Consent)

When you create an account or sign in with Google, we ask for your explicit consent before saving your marks, calculating your APS, or bookmarking bursaries. We never process learner information without your awareness and agreement.

2

Collect Only What We Need (Minimal Data)

We only request details strictly required to calculate your university eligibility and track funding schemes:

  • Matric Subject Marks: To calculate your official DBE APS score and faculty eligibility.
  • Current Grade & Province: To filter province-specific bursaries (e.g., Gauteng City Region Academy).
  • School Name & Study Interests: To guide relevant vocational degrees and TVET career paths.
  • Email Address: To allow you to log in securely across phone and desktop.
3

Keep Data Safe & Secure (Enterprise Safeguards)

All data transmission between your phone and our servers is encrypted using modern SSL/TLS (HTTPS). Account credentials are protected using salted PBKDF2 with SHA-256 password hashing. We never sell your personal information or contact details to third-party marketers or advertisers.

4

Give You Complete Control (Your POPIA Rights)

Under POPIA Sections 23, 24, and 25, you retain full rights over your personal information:

  • Right to Access: View all saved marks, applications, and bookmarks directly on your Profile.
  • Right to Correction: Update your grade, school, or subjects anytime.
  • Right to Erasure (Deletion): Request permanent deletion of your profile and submitted records by contacting our Information Officer.

Information Officer Contact

If you have any questions about how your data is handled, wish to request a copy of your records, or want your account permanently erased, please contact:
