## Slide 1 (Element 01): Identify the User, Problem & Strong Supporting Insight
- **Target User:** Grade 10–12 learners and matriculants across South Africa, especially in under-resourced public schools.
- **The Core Problem:** Learners lack accessible, personalized academic counseling. Fragmented bursary portals and complex APS formulas lead to missed university deadlines and rejected applications.
- **Supporting Insight:** South African public schools face a **1 : 1,000+** guidance counselor-to-learner deficit, directly contributing to a **~30% first-year university dropout rate** driven by mismatched degree selection and funding gaps.

---

## Slide 2 (Element 02): The App, Core Purpose & User Journey
- **Core Purpose:** A mobile-first, autonomous AI guidance counselor that bridges matriculants from high school into fully funded tertiary education.
- **Concise 4-Step User Journey:**
  1. **Discover:** 6-question career quiz pinpoints student strengths and RIASEC career archetypes.
  2. **Calculate:** Dual-mode DBE Level 1–7 / percentage APS calculator delivers instant, error-free university scores.
  3. **Match & Consult:** Real-time streaming AI Counselor recommends qualifying university degrees and TVET pathways.
  4. **Fund & Track:** Direct bursary tracker monitors live deadlines, coverage criteria, and application stages.

---

## Slide 3 (Element 03): Difference from Existing Alternatives
- **Existing Alternatives:** Static 100-page university PDF prospectuses, disconnected government portals, and ad-cluttered job boards.
- **PfunzoBridge Key Differences:**
  - **Interactive AI vs Static PDFs:** Conversational counseling tailored to actual student marks vs deciphering complex tables.
  - **Automated DBE Scoring:** Official South African APS conversion built-in—no manual math or errors.
  - **Single Pane of Glass:** Integrates career discovery, university admission requirements, and bursary tracking in one app.
  - **POPIA-Compliant & Low-Data:** 100% ad-free, strict data privacy, and optimized for low-bandwidth mobile connections.

---

## Slide 4 (Element 04): How It Works & Creates Impact
- **Technology Stack:**
  - **Frontend:** Responsive, mobile-first PWA interface (Tailwind CSS, offline-friendly).
  - **Backend:** Python 3.14 + Django 5.1 with Server-Sent Events (SSE) streaming architecture.
  - **AI Engine:** Google Antigravity reasoning engine grounded in South African curriculum standards (NSC / DBE).
- **Measurable Impact:**
  - Democratizes 1-on-1 career guidance for any learner with a smartphone.
  - Prevents missed deadlines by surfacing active bursaries before cutoff dates.
  - Production-ready: 22 automated test suites passing with zero security vulnerabilities.

## Slide 6 (Element 06): Business Model / Sustainability
- **Guiding Principle:** **100% Free and Ad-Free for Students** — education access should never be paywalled.
- **Sustainable B2B Revenue Model:**
  1. **Higher Education Recruitment:** Universities subscribe to talent discovery pipelines for qualified matriculants in scarce STEM disciplines.
  2. **Corporate Bursary Sponsor Portals:** Corporate CSI and bursary trusts pay for verified applicant matching to fulfill B-BBEE Skills Development scorecards.
  3. **Aggregated Education Insights:** Anonymized regional trends on subject performance and career intent licensed to education planners and NGOs.
